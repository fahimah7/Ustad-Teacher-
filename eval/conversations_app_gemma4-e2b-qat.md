The concept on this page is about the average rate of change (تغییر متوسط). When you have a function, the average rate of change is simply the change in the function's value ($\Delta y$) divided by the change in the input ($\Delta x$). This is calculated using the formula: $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(x_2) - f(x_1)}{x_2 - x_1}$. Think of it like finding the average speed when you travel between two points.

The limit (لیمت) of this ratio as the change in $x$ approaches zero gives you the instantaneous rate of change (سرعت لحظه‌ای). For example, in the first example, for the function $f(x) = x^2$ on the interval $[2, 5]$, we calculate $\frac{f(5) - f(2)}{5 - 2} = \frac{25 - 4}{3} = \frac{21}{3} = 7$.
