The average change of a function $y = f(x)$ over an interval $[x_1, x_2]$ is calculated as the ratio of the change in the function's value ($\Delta y$) to the change in the input variable ($\Delta x$): $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(x_2) - f(x_1)}{x_2 - x_1}$. This is essentially finding the slope of the secant line between the two points. The limit of this ratio as $\Delta x$ approaches zero gives you the slope of the tangent line, which is the instantaneous rate of change (مشتق) at a point.     $$\Delta y = 2(x^2 + 2x\Delta x + (\Delta x)^2) - 4 - 2x^2 + 4$$
    $$\Delta y = 2x^2 + 4x\Delta x + 2(\Delta x)^2 - 2x^2 = 4x\Delta x + 2(\Delta x)^2$$
2.  **Find the ratio $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$**:
    $$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{4x\Delta x + 2(\Delta x)^2}{\Delta x} = 4x + 2\Delta x$$

    $$\text{میل} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} (4x + 2\Delta x) = 4x$$

So, the ratio is $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = 4x + 2\Delta x$, and the limit (میل) at $x = 0$ is $0$.
