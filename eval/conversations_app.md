**Ustad** (5.4 s):

This page explains the concept of average change (تغییر متوسط) using the idea of movement. If an object moves along a straight line, the distance traveled is a function of time, $S = f(t)$. The average speed between times $t_1$ and $t_2$ is the change in distance divided by the change in time: $\frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t} = \frac{f(t_2) - f(t_1)}{t_2 - t_1}$. The limit of this ratio as the time interval approaches zero gives the instantaneous speed at a specific time $t_0$. For a function, the average change is the ratio of the change in the function's value ($\Delta y$) to the change in the input ($\Delta x$): $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(x_2) - f(x_1)}{x_2 - x_1}$.

Can you calculate the average change for the function $y = x^2$ on the interval $[2, 5]$?
