$\Delta y = f(x + \Delta x) - f(x) = [2(x + \Delta x)^2 - 4] - [2x^2 - 4]$
$\Delta y = 2(x^2 + 2x\Delta x + (\Delta x)^2) - 4 - 2x^2 + 4$

$\Delta y = 4x\Delta x + 2(\Delta x)^2$

$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{4x\Delta x + 2(\Delta x)^2}{\Delta x} = 4x + 2\Delta x$

Finally, to find the slope (میل منحنی), we take the limit as $\Delta x$ approaches 0:
$\text{Slope} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} (4x + 2\Delta x) = 4x$
At $x = 0$, the slope is $4(0) = 0$.

میل در $x=0$ برابر $0$ است.
