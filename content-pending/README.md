questions are not complete, so they are kept out of the app. To finish one, move its folder back to
`content/` and continue with pipeline/TRANSCRIBE.md (the pages that already have notes are skipped),
then pipeline/FINISH_BOOK.md. Page images are links to D:\ustad-content.
