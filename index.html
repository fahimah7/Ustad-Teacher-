Questions

?
Is Ustad free?
Yes. Every grade, every subject, every feature. No ads.
?
Do I need the internet?
Only to download. After that, lessons, search, practice and Ustad's answers all work offline. The app never connects to the internet.
?
Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for: no name, no email, no phone number. Your progress stays on your device.
?
Which languages?
Dari, Pashto and English, in the app and in the lessons. More languages will follow.
?
Where do the lessons come from?
From the official school textbooks, rebuilt into short lessons and checked by teachers. Every lesson shows which textbook pages it covers.
